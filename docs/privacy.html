Studento Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Studento is a school clock app. It runs entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Studento does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising.

What stays on your device

  • Your schedule, class names and rooms
  • Your settings and theme
  • Notes, grades, homework and focus-timer history you enter

This information is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Photos

If you scan your schedule, Studento reads the photo you choose using your device's built-in text recognition. The photo and the text found in it are processed on your device and are never uploaded.

Notifications

Bell alerts are scheduled on your device. Studento does not use push notifications and has no server.

Network

Studento does not contact any server of ours. The app may check with Apple's and Expo's standard app-update services, which do not receive your schedule or settings.

Children

Studento is made for students. Because it collects no data, there is nothing to share, sell or delete.
